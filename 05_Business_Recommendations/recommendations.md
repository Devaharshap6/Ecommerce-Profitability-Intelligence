# Business Recommendations

## 1. Prioritize freight optimization

Freight is a significant source of contribution leakage. Management should identify categories and sellers where freight represents an unusually high percentage of revenue.

## 2. Focus on Electronics

Electronics shows a particularly high freight burden relative to revenue and a low modeled contribution margin.

The analysis estimates:

- Revenue: approximately ₹155K
- Freight: approximately ₹45.7K
- Freight / Revenue: 29.46%
- Modeled Contribution Margin: approximately 5.5%

A modeled 10% freight reduction would add approximately ₹4,568 in contribution.

## 3. Evaluate sellers using contribution, not revenue alone

High-revenue sellers can still produce weak economics when freight costs are disproportionately high.

Seller evaluation should therefore include:

- Revenue
- Freight
- Modeled contribution
- Contribution margin
- Order volume

## 4. Investigate shipping routes

Product weight alone does not explain the observed Electronics freight leakage.

Further investigation should examine:

- Seller location
- Customer location
- Shipping distance
- Carrier pricing
- Packaging
- Product dimensions

## 5. Connect delivery performance with customer experience

Late delivery rates should be monitored alongside review scores.

High-volume sellers or routes with both elevated delays and poor reviews should receive operational attention.

## 6. Improve the profitability model

The current 65% product-cost assumption is a modeling assumption rather than an accounting value.

For production use, replace it with actual:

- Product acquisition cost
- Packaging cost
- Payment processing cost
- Fulfillment cost
- Return/refund cost

This would make the contribution analysis substantially more accurate.

## Business Priority

The immediate opportunity is to investigate high-volume categories and sellers where freight consumes a disproportionate share of revenue.

The objective is not simply to increase sales, but to improve the amount of contribution retained from each order.
